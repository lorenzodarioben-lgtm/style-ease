# Art direction and critique evidence

The frame is fashion as composition: a visible geometric STYLE EASE identity, dramatic monochrome campaign, large DM Sans contrasted with Bodoni, then bone surfaces where shoppers need to compare choices. Editorial inversions separate discovery from task-focused shopping. The house mark, precise crops and large footer wordmark recur without fake edition numbers or endorsements.

Real captures live in screenshots/baseline and screenshots/overhaul. Compare home-1440.png and home-390.png in each folder, then shop/product/bag-empty. Captures are rendered from the actual apps; the overhaul talks to real local PostgreSQL. The capture script decodes below-fold lazy images before photographing a full page.

## Critique round one

Top defects: intrinsic HTML image heights stretched the outfit invitation and distorted page rhythm; native selects forced narrow grids wider than their containers; dynamic content landmarks/headings needed correction. Also full-page screenshots initially captured unloaded lazy images, so those first images did not represent final content. Fixed all four, then recaptured all five device classes.

## Critique round two

The repaired composition maintains clear brand/navigation and aligned shopping surfaces; full-page 1440 and 390 captures were inspected. The second 50-screen sweep recorded no overflow, axe findings on scanned routes or JS errors. The next functional critique exposed HTMX v4's event.detail.ctx contract and dynamic filter label addressing; both repaired and their four affected browser cases passed. This is verification of named fixes, not a claim of perfect design.

Intentionally omitted: disconnected brand logo wall, manufactured review ratings, badges/discounts, sustainability or shipping promises, ornamental gradients, cursor effects, scroll hijacking and invented product angles. Remaining constraint: the seed catalogue's photographs are editorial references rather than a verified product photography library.
