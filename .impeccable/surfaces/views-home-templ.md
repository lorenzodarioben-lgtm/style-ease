# Creative rescue direction

Kinetic monochrome editorialism is the user's chosen world. The code-led prototypes use actual licensed photographs and executable HTML/CSS, not generated mockup substitutes. Audience: fashion-conscious browsers and portfolio reviewers; scene: a gallery-like desktop experience and a clear, deliberate phone shopping flow. The commerce task remains immediate.

## Direction contract

SEED KEY: user-pinned-black-label-creative-v2. Code-led; existing house and explicit user brief govern the direction.

THESIS: clothing as spatial composition, with restrained monochrome opening into genuine photographic colour. Replace repeated beige grids and arbitrary portrait-to-landscape crops with an intentional fashion publication and visual atelier.

OWN-WORLD: deep ink, ivory, graphite and a restrained mineral tonal family. A custom typographic identity; proportion-led media frames; oversized but legible editorial lettering, quiet shopping typography and square, sliding-fill controls.

STORY: enter a campaign, shop an edit, read an authored visual essay, then compose and save a private outfit. Collections sell a curated selection; Editorial invites sustained reading; Studio is a photographic workspace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: render two executable alternatives at 1440/390 before choosing. Both reserve complete subject bounds and an unmistakable shop action; no text over faces, masked defects or compressed desktop layout.

FORM: the brief pins the direction and explicitly delegates concept selection. Three identity alternatives and two page compositions will be compared through actual browser screenshots. Code-led implementation; no inferred permission or extra approval round is required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

DESIGN_VARIANCE 8 / MOTION_INTENSITY 6 / VISUAL_DENSITY 3 for discovery; lower motion and higher task density for commerce. Pointer, focus, touch, no-JS and reduced-motion behavior ship together. The brief's five critique rounds and autonomous selection govern this task.

## Rendered selection

The identity sheet compares lowercase optical lettering (A), architectural capitals (B) and serif/slash masthead (C) at header, light/dark, 16/32/56px favicon and footer sizes. Choose A: the counter shapes, descender and compact word spacing form a coherent, readable house signature. B is legible but too close to the existing utility typography; C adds an unnecessary slash and reads like a publication title rather than the store name. This is original arrangement/outline work from licensed Bodoni Moda, not a claim to have invented the underlying typeface. FontTools 4.67.0 is an offline utility outside the repo/runtime.

Homepage A preserves the complete campaign photograph; B is an ink diptych with separate full-source portraits. A is stronger in full-scale photography and clearer as a commerce entrance. Its initial four-line headline overpowers the phone; shorten to a deliberately two-line statement before production. B's staggered portraits are useful language for the later Studio invitation, but its first fold is too fragmented. Actual alternatives are retained in evidence/concepts at 1440 and 390.
