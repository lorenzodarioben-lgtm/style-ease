# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People browsing a modern fashion catalogue on desktop or mobile. They need to discover relevant styles, compare choices, select variants, and complete a clearly simulated purchase without losing their browser-local selections.

## Product Purpose

Style Ease demonstrates a complete, accessible front-end storefront flow. Its success is measured by a confident path from discovery through variant selection, bag review, and a transparent demo checkout.

## Brand Personality

Architectural, confident, and precise. The experience should make monochrome fashion feel considered and easy to navigate, without becoming intimidating or overly ornamental.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace clutter, deceptive scarcity, faux-account features, and decorative effects that distract from shopping decisions. Do not imply real payment processing, fulfilment, or live inventory.

## Design Principles

- Make every step from discovery to checkout easy to understand and reverse.
- Let product information and imagery carry the visual impact.
- Keep choices explicit, especially size, colour, quantity, and availability.
- Preserve shopper context across routes and browser sessions.
- Treat accessibility as part of the shopping experience, not an add-on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic controls, keyboard operation, visible focus, live feedback, reduced-motion support, and responsive behaviour. Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction expectations while retaining the app's high-contrast visual language.
